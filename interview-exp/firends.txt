Asn: Node's Buffer limit. A Buffer can't hold 100GB. The max is buffer.constants.MAX_LENGTH — around 2GB on 32-bit and up to ~4GB (or Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER-bounded but practically capped) on 64-bit builds. You'll get an error like ERR_FS_FILE_TOO_LARGE before you even run out of RAM.
correct:
const readStream = fs.createReadStream("100GB.log");
let totalLength = 0;
readStream.on("data", (chunk)=> {
  totalLength += chunk.length;
})
readStream.on("end", ()=> {
  console.log(totalLength);
})
